Burrow Privacy Policy

Nikka Studio · Effective 6 November 2026

In short: Burrow does not collect, send or share any data. The app has no internet access. Everything you put in Burrow stays on your phone, encrypted.

What Burrow does

Burrow is a private vault for photos and videos. You choose files to add, and Burrow stores an encrypted copy of them inside the app on your device.

Data we collect

None. Burrow does not ask for the Internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your device. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no crash reporting and no third-party tracking.

Your photos and videos

  • Files you add are encrypted on your device (AES-256-GCM) and kept in the app's private storage.
  • Your PIN is never stored. It is used only on your device to unlock the encryption key.
  • Fingerprint unlock is handled by Android. Burrow never sees or stores your fingerprint.
  • Burrow opts out of Android cloud backup, so vault data is not copied to Google Drive or any other service.

Permissions

  • Photo and video picker: you pick files through Android's own picker, or share them to Burrow from another app. Burrow only gets the files you choose.
  • Storage (Android 8 and 9 only): used only when you choose to save a file back to your gallery.
  • Biometrics: used only if you turn on fingerprint unlock.

Sharing

Burrow shares a file only when you tap Share, and only with the app you choose.

Deleting your data

Deleting a file in Burrow removes it from the vault. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes all vault data permanently. Because nothing is kept anywhere else, we cannot recover it, and a forgotten PIN cannot be reset.

Children

Burrow is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
